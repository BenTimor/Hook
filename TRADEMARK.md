# Trademark policy

The code in this repository is licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public
License v3.0** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). That license grants you broad rights to
the *code*. It grants you no rights to the *name*.

This file explains the difference. It is deliberately short, and it is the part
that actually stops someone reselling Hook under Hook's name.

## What is covered

The following are trademarks of the project owner, and are **not** licensed by
the AGPL:

- The names **Hook**, **Hook Workspace** and **HookCode**
- The Hook mark and logo, in any form, including the icon sets under
  `apps/*/src-tauri/icons/` and `packages/ui/`
- The domain **hookcode.io** and any confusingly similar name or mark
- Product artwork, screenshots and store listings

## What you may do

You may, without asking:

- Use the code under the terms of the AGPL.
- State, factually and in plain text, that your project **is based on** Hook or
  **is a fork of** Hook. For example: *"Forked from Hook —
  https://github.com/BenTimor/Hook"*.
- Keep the existing copyright, license and attribution notices intact. You must
  do this in any case; see the AGPL.
- Write about Hook, review it, compare it to other software, and teach with it.

## What you may not do

You may not, without written permission:

- Use the name Hook, HookCode or Hook Workspace, or the Hook mark, as the name
  of your fork, product, company, service, package, app listing or domain.
- Use the mark in a way that suggests your fork is official, endorsed by, or
  produced by the Hook project.
- Distribute a modified build under the Hook name or mark. **A fork must
  rebrand.**
- Publish a Microsoft Store, App Store, Play Store or package-manager listing
  under a Hook name or mark.
- Register a domain, social handle or package name that is confusingly similar
  to Hook, HookCode or hookcode.io.

## Why this matters

The AGPL makes reselling the code unattractive by requiring a reseller to
publish their modifications. It does not stop them from calling the result
"Hook".

This policy does. It is the reason a competitor has to rebuild your reputation
from scratch rather than inheriting it.

## If you want to use the name

Ask. If you are running an unmodified, self-hosted instance inside your own
organisation, you are already fine — no permission needed. If you want to
distribute a fork publicly, rebrand it, and then there is nothing to ask.

Contact: **support@hookcode.io**
