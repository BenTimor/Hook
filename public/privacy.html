Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Hook is an early-access desktop app for working with AI. This policy covers hookcode.io — the landing page and the early-access waitlist — and nothing else.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, you sign in with Google and we store three things:

  • the verified email address on your Google Account;
  • a stable Google account identifier (the OpenID sub value), so that joining twice does not create two entries;
  • the date and time you joined.

We do not receive your Google password, your contacts, your files, or any other data from your Google Account. We do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on this site.

Cookies

The waitlist sets two first-party cookies, and nothing else:

  • hook_flow — lives for 10 minutes and protects the Google sign-in round trip against forgery;
  • hook_joined — lives for 7 days and remembers that you are already on the list so the page can say so.

Both are HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax. There are no third-party cookies and no cookie banner, because there is nothing to consent to beyond this.

Why we keep it

Your email address is used for exactly one purpose: to tell you when early access to Hook opens, and to send the few messages that come with it (invite, and anything that changes the invite). We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone, and we do not add you to any other list.

Where it lives

The waitlist is a single SQLite database on a server we operate. Google's sign-in itself is covered by Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Until early access ends or you ask us to remove it — whichever comes first. When the waitlist is no longer needed, the database is deleted.

Removing your data

Email nsgi3111@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your entry. There is no account to close and no other data to remove.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it. Write to the address above and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

Hook is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be announced to the address you signed up with.
