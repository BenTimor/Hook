Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Hook is an early-access desktop app for working with AI. This policy covers hookcode.io — the landing page and the early-access waitlist — and nothing else.

What we collect

If you join the waitlist, you sign in with Google and we store:

  • the verified email address on your Google Account;
  • a stable Google account identifier (the OpenID sub value), so that joining twice does not create two entries;
  • your name, if Google provides it;
  • the interests you select and your preference for a future Hook plan, including its displayed price and billing period;
  • your onboarding selections and changes as you make them, even if you leave before finishing;
  • the date and time you joined, your current onboarding step, completion time if you finish, and your waitlist status.

We do not receive your Google password, your contacts, your files, or any other data from your Google Account beyond the identifier, email, and optional name described above. We record your onboarding choices directly to understand what people want from Hook. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising scripts.

Cookies

The site sets two first-party cookies:

  • hook_flow — lives for 10 minutes and protects the Google sign-in round trip against forgery;
  • hook_joined — lives for 7 days and associates your onboarding progress with your Google sign-in.

Both are HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax. There are no third-party cookies set by Hook.

Your browser also uses session storage to remember your onboarding choices while you move between screens. This stores your selected interests, plan preference, and onboarding step, and is cleared when your browser session ends. Choices that have not yet reached our server are also kept in local storage, associated with your Google account identifier, so they can be retried when you return with the same sign-in. They are removed after a successful save.

Why we keep it

Your email address is used for exactly one purpose: to tell you when early access to Hook opens, and to send the few messages that come with it (invite, and anything that changes the invite). We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone, and we do not add you to any other list.

Your interests, plan preference, and partial onboarding progress help us understand what early members want to create with Hook and where they stop during signup. Choosing a plan records a preference and does not create a paid subscription.

Where it lives

The waitlist is a single SQLite database on a server we operate. Google's sign-in itself is covered by Google's privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Until early access ends or you ask us to remove it — whichever comes first. When the waitlist is no longer needed, the database is deleted.

Removing your data

Email nsgi3111@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your entry. There is no account to close and no other data to remove.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it. Write to the address above and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

Hook is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be announced to the address you signed up with.
