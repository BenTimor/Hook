# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Hook to be a good experience for anyone who shows up in
good faith, regardless of experience level, background, identity or what they do
for a living.

## Our standards

Things that make this a better place:

- Being direct about code and kind about people. Review a patch, not a person.
- Assuming competence. Someone who wrote something odd usually had a reason —
  ask what it was before rewriting it.
- Accepting that "this doesn't fit the architecture" is a real answer, and it is
  not personal.
- Giving a newcomer a pointer to the file or the doc that answers their
  question, instead of just a verdict.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or bad-faith argument.
- Sexualised language or imagery in project spaces.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any
space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Email **support@hookcode.io**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential. Say what happened, who was involved, and whether it is ongoing. If
you are not comfortable naming someone, say so — the report still matters.

Maintainers are obligated to keep reporters' identities confidential.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and history, a response may be:

1. A private word about what was wrong.
2. A public correction or an apology.
3. A temporary ban from the project's spaces.
4. A permanent ban.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this
code, and will explain why. Decisions are final.
