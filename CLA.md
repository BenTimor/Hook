## Corporate Contributor License Agreement

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer, or if your employment
agreement gives your employer rights to your work, a **Corporate CLA** is
required instead of the individual one. It is the same grant as above, executed
by an authorized signatory of the entity, and lists the individuals covered.

Email **support@hookcode.io** to request the current corporate form.

## How to sign

Open a pull request. The CLA bot comments with what to do; reply with the
signing phrase from [CLA.md](CLA.md) and the signature is recorded in
`.github/cla-signatures.json` by the workflow. No email is needed for the
individual CLA.

A **corporate CLA** is emailed to **support@hookcode.io** instead. Once it is on
file, add the person's GitHub login to `.github/cla-allowlist.json` so the gate
stops asking them.
